# 📚 Exam Schedule Assistant AI Agent

An AI-powered web application that helps students prepare for exams by generating personalized study timetables, sending exam reminders, and tracking study progress.

## 🚀 Overview

The **Exam Schedule Assistant** is designed to make exam preparation easier, smarter, and more organized. Students can enter their subjects, exam dates, available study hours, and preparation levels to receive a personalized study plan.

The AI agent helps students manage their time effectively, prioritize important subjects, and monitor their preparation progress.

## ✨ Features

- **🤖 AI-Powered Study Planning:** Generate personalized study schedules based on subjects, exam dates, and available study time.
- **📅 Automatic Timetable Generation:** Organize daily study sessions and revision periods automatically.
- **⏰ Exam Reminders:** Get reminders about upcoming exams and planned study sessions.
- **📊 Progress Tracking:** Track completed study sessions, topics covered, and overall preparation progress.
- **📚 Subject Management:** Add subjects, topics, exam dates, and preparation priorities.
- **🔄 Flexible Scheduling:** Adjust study plans when schedules change or tasks are incomplete.
- **💻 User-Friendly Dashboard:** View upcoming exams, today's tasks, and study progress in one place.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Reduce exam preparation stress through better planning.
- Help students prioritize subjects according to deadlines and preparation needs.
- Encourage consistent study habits.
- Improve time management and revision planning.
- Provide an organized view of exam readiness.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Enter your subjects and exam dates.
2. Add the topics you need to study.
3. Specify your available study hours and preparation level.
4. Generate a personalized timetable using AI.
5. Follow daily study tasks and revision sessions.
6. Mark tasks as completed and track your progress.
7. Review upcoming exams and reminders.

## 🖥️ Main Modules

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Displays upcoming exams and daily study tasks |
| Subject Manager | Manages subjects, topics, and exam dates |
| AI Timetable Generator | Creates a personalized study plan |
| Reminder System | Reminds students about exams and study sessions |
| Progress Tracker | Tracks completed tasks and preparation progress |
